TransLume — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Short version: TransLume collects no data, has no account, shows no ads, and contains no tracking or analytics of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. TransLume does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. There is no sign-up, no account, and the app works entirely offline.

Your artwork and photos

Images you import into TransLume stay on your iPad, inside the app's private storage. They are never uploaded anywhere. TransLume uses the system photo picker, which grants the app access only to the single image you chose — never your whole photo library. Removing a photo in the app deletes it from the app's storage.

Settings

Your preferences (brightness level, color temperature, handedness, and the scale, position and opacity you set per image) are stored only on your device using iOS's standard local storage. They never leave your iPad.

Third parties

TransLume contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no analytics, and no tracking pixels. It requests no photo-library or camera permissions beyond the system picker described above.

Children

Since no data of any kind is collected, no data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date above will change and the updated policy will ship with an app update.
