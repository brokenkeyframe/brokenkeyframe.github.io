TransLume · Tracing lightbox for artists · by Simas Gineika

Something not behaving the way it should? Everything in TransLume works offline on your iPad — there is no account to get locked out of and nothing to reset on a server. That usually makes problems easy to fix right here.

Quick fixes

The screen isn't bright enough

TransLume's slider sets your iPad's actual display brightness. If it still looks dim, check Settings → Display & Brightness, and note that Auto-Brightness (Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size) can cap things in dark rooms.

A photo didn't appear

Import uses the standard iOS photo picker — it only sees the image you tap. If an import seems missing, try importing again and confirm you can see it in the library grid (photo button on the capsule).

The controls moved or disappeared

The floating capsule can be dragged to either side and mirrors automatically for left-handed use (Settings sheet → Handedness). If Lock mode is on, tap the lock button first.

App updates wiped my references

Normal updates never remove imported images. Deleting the app does, though — iOS removes app documents then, and they can't be recovered from our side because we never have a copy.

Contact
